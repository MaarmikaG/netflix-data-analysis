 ## 📊 Netflix Data Analysis Project

## 🔹 Objective

To analyze the Netflix dataset and gain insights into the platform's content strategy, popular genres, production trends, and the evolution of streaming content over time.

## 🔹 Technologies Used

* **Python**
* **Pandas** – Data manipulation and analysis using DataFrames
* **NumPy** – Numerical computations and array operations
* **Matplotlib** – Data visualization and plotting
* **Seaborn** – Statistical data visualization
* **Jupyter Notebook** – Interactive coding and visualization environment


## 🔹 Key Insights

* Netflix has been consistently adding more shows and movies each year, showing strong content growth.
* The platform offers more movies than TV shows, but both categories are steadily increasing.
* Most content falls under **TV-MA** and **TV-14**, showing a focus on mature and teen audiences.
* The **United States** leads in content production, followed by countries like **India, UK, and Canada**.
* Popular movie genres include **Drama, Comedy, and Documentaries**.
* Popular TV show categories include **International TV Shows, Crime TV Shows, and TV Dramas**.


## 🔹 Conclusion

This analysis highlights Netflix's rapid expansion and strategic focus on diverse and mature content. The platform continues to grow globally, with strong contributions from multiple countries and a wide range of genres catering to different audience preferences.
